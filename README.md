## 3. Governance Analysis: "Is this the Director's qualified duty to do it?"

Under Pakistani statutory law and Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh), the answer requires distinguishing between **Preparation (Managerial)** and **Approval (Fiduciary Authority)**:

### A. Preparation & Compilation (YES — Qualified Duty of the Director / Administrator)
- The **Director / Administrator** is directly responsible for day-to-day operations: supervising staff, monitoring FESCO/SNGPL utility bills, managing Madrasa rations, purchasing wudu supplies, and keeping logbooks.
- Operationally, the Director is the **most qualified person to draft and construct the budget** because they have empirical access to monthly consumption patterns, inflation figures, and operational bottlenecks.

### B. Approval, Enactment & Legal Liability (NO — Reserved for the Board of Trustees / Shura)
- The Director **cannot unilaterally approve, sign into law, or execute the budget without Board sanction**.
- **Punjab Charities Act, 2019 (PCC):** Registered non-profit charities in Punjab must have their annual budgets formally adopted by the governing body (Board of Trustees / Managing Committee) during an official meeting with recorded minutes.
- **Societies Registration Act, 1860 / Trust Act:** The Treasurer and President bear collective fiduciary liability. An administrator who spends outside a board-approved budget faces personal civil and criminal liability for breach of trust (*Khiyanat*).

### C. Shariah Governance (Zakat & Waqf Restriction Rules)
Mosque finances differ from commercial enterprises because funds are sacred trusts (*Amanat*):
1. **Zakat & Fitrana:** Must follow **Tamleek** (Surah At-Tawbah 9:60) — direct transfer of ownership to verified poor Muslims (*Mustahiqeen*), such as orphan or needy students of the Madrasa, widows, and destitute families in Joharabad. **Zakat cannot be used to pay electricity bills, paint walls, or pay the general Imam's salary.**
2. **Waqf Income:** Rental income from the 4 commercial shops endowed to the Masjid must first be utilized to preserve and maintain the Waqf property and the physical Masjid structure.
3. **General Sadaqah & Atiyyat:** Available for unrestricted operational expenses.

> **Conclusion:** The Director has the **qualified duty to prepare and recommend** the budget, but **must submit it to the Treasurer for audit, the Shariah Advisor for Zakat vetting, and the Board of Trustees for formal legal approval**.
